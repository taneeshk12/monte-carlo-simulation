A neural network that hedges options, beside the textbook

You have sold a 30-day call option. Each day you choose how many shares to hold so a price move can't hurt you. The textbook (Black-Scholes) rebalances to an exact number every day; the AI learned that trading costs money and trades less. Move the dials to change the market.

How it works

Real data

20 years of S&P 500 (SPY) and VIX, plus Bitcoin prices, DVOL and every expired option on Deribit.

A realistic fake market

A Heston model with jumps (Bates) is fitted to that data and generates 200,000 practice months.

Training

A small network picks the shares to hold each day, punished by its average loss in the worst 5% of months, after costs.

Honest testing

Compared with Black-Scholes on new fake months, real SPY history and real traded Bitcoin options.

Based on Buehler et al., Deep Hedging (2019). The network in this page is the real trained model, running in your browser. Code: github.com/taneeshk12/quant-projects
